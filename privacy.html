Apollo privacy policy

Apollo is a private app on its owner's home PC with a single user, its owner.

With the owner's permission, Apollo uses Google's Smart Device Management API to list the owner's Nest cameras, show their live video on the owner's own TV and phone, and receive sound and person alerts. Video and audio stream from Google directly to the owner's devices; Apollo does not record or store them.

Google's sign-in token is kept only on the owner's PC and can be removed at any time with Disconnect in Apollo or from Google Account permissions. No data is sold, shared, or used for ads or analytics.
